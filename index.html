Research What I Work On

My research focuses on certifiable autonomy for intelligent aerospace systems. My work till date has been centered on robust control which acts as a basis for uncertanity aware planning and control with high confidence. I validate algorithms on aerospace testbeds, bridging the gap between theoretical guarantees and real hardware deployments across multiple technology readiness levels.
More recently, I have been exploring the learning-to-plan paradigm, combining transformer-based sequence models, reinforcement learning, and Gaussian Process Finite State Machines (GP-FSM) to enable long-horizon decision-making through learned abstractions and heuristics. My work sits at the intersection of algorithmic rigor and physical implementation — I am equally at home running simulations as I am wiring up sensors and running tests on hardware.

Learning to Plan

Learning-based planners that use sequence models and ML to learn planning structure, abstractions, and heuristics enabling faster long-horizon decision-making in complex environments.

Certifiable Autonomy

Developing robust, uncertainty-aware control and planning methods with safety and performance guarantees for reliable learning-enabled autonomy beyond idealized assumptions.

Hardware Validation

Bridging simulation and real aerospace testbeds through real-time implementation, sensing, integration, and experimental validation.

Resilient Systems

Building autonomous systems that remain dependable under disturbances, model mismatch, sensor degradation, and failures by combining robust control, and uncertainty quantification.

Projects

Selected Work

A selection of research projects, tools, and experiments I have built or contributed to.

Variable Pitch Quadrotor with Slung Payload

ISR Lab, Texas A&M University

Developing a variable pitch quadrotor UAV optimized for long-endurance missions with slung payload transport, integrating robust control on PX4 autopilots validated in high-fidelity simulation.

Robust Control PX4 UAV Hardware Path Planning

FPGA-Based GNSS Receiver

IGCL, IIT Kanpur

Built an FPGA-based GNSS receiver with robust signal acquisition under high dynamic loads, featuring custom IP cores for Xilinx and Effinix FPGAs with reduced resource footprint.

FPGA GNSS Embedded Systems Signal Processing

ℋ∞ Control of Biplane Quadrotor Tailsitter

IGCL, IIT Kanpur

Applied ℋ∞ robust control theory to guarantee stability under physical uncertainties for a VTOL tailsitter UAV, validated in Gazebo SITL and hardware-in-the-loop using MATLAB.

ℋ∞ Control VTOL Gazebo SITL MATLAB
